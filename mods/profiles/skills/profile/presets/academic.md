# Preset: academic

**Signals:** `*.tex`, `*.bib`, `*.lyx`, `*.pptx`, a thesis, lab report or homework folder.

**Relevant skills:** academic writing and style, LaTeX and LyX, citations, presentations, homework solutions, statistics, humanize and English grammar, Word and PDF files.

**Commands to look for:** `latexmk -xelatex`, `lyx --export pdf`, a Makefile target.

**Permissions:** allow LaTeX builds; ask before overwriting submitted files.

**Extras:** never invent citations; verify every reference.
