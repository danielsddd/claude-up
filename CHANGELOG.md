# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-23)

First public release.

- **damn-ddd:** an animated show behind the chat when one reply runs 10 minutes: damn1, a cowboy whipping a bucket of tokens, and damn2, an anime hero cutting an evil token in two. They take turns and leave on a click. Zero model tokens.
- **damn-ddd tools:** the ink + flat-color tracer that turns hand-drawn art into backdrop-sized SVG, with the source images.
